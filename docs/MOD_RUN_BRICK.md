Replacing the `audreybrick` folder no longer deletes anything in `saves/audreybrick/`. Updated 2026-11-21 for MOD-C (four ramp shapes, a POLARITY row, the envelope following the input before the gain). **Updated again 2026-11-21 for MOD-D** (your new gestures: a TARGET row instead of SLOT, hold X to pick the slot on the cards, no target list). **The MOD-D package is not yet run.**

## What is new

A seventh tab, **MOD**, after INPUT (L1 from LOOP goes straight to it). Four modulation slots, each with a shape (SINE, TRIANGLE, SQUARE, RAMP UP, RAMP DOWN, EXP UP, EXP DOWN, S&H, ENVELOPE), a rate (0.010 HZ to 2.00 KHZ; for ENVELOPE the row is RELEASE, 5.00 S to 5.00 MS), a depth (-100 % to +100 %), a polarity (BIPOLAR: both ways round the knob; UNIPOLAR: only to one side; ENVELOPE is always unipolar) and one target (any knob, INPUT GAIN or DELAY SWITCH). New since your first run: the four ramps, the POLARITY row, and the ENVELOPE now follows the input **before** the INPUT tab's gain (it works with the gain at -inf dB). The top five rows (TARGET, SHAPE, RATE, DEPTH, POLARITY) edit the selected slot; the four cards below are the four slots, each with a small bar that moves with what the slot does. New in MOD-D: the cards are dimmed while you edit the rows, and the selected one has a bright frame and number; **hold X** and the cards light up (the rows dim) and left / right pick the slot. TARGET steps through the 14 targets with left / right (it goes round from DELAY SWITCH to NONE); the target list is gone. On every other tab a modulated knob gets a violet tag (`M1`, `M13`) and a thin violet line on its bar where the modulation has moved it. Settings last until you quit; nothing is saved yet.

The buttons on MOD:

| Buttons | Does |
|---|---|
| Up / down | the row: TARGET, SHAPE, RATE, DEPTH, POLARITY |
| Left / right | the row's value for the selected slot (TARGET steps round the list and repeats when held, 8 per second; RATE and DEPTH ramp when held); SELECT held: fine; on POLARITY right UNIPOLAR, left BIPOLAR |
| X held + left / right | select the slot 1 to 4 (round from 4 to 1), one per press; the rows keep their place |
| Y + left / right | the row to its default (on TARGET: NONE) |
| Y + X + left / right | the whole selected slot back to its start |

What the device has to tell us: whether fast modulation of several slots at once costs too much (crackle, the CPU meter), whether the envelope follows the microphone, USB and file inputs usefully (now also with the gain at -inf dB), whether the ramps and the polarity are useful, and whether the five rows are readable and the steps feel right.

## Before you start

Headphones (several steps use the microphone). A USB audio interface if you have it, and a few WAV files in `data/input` as in the earlier input runs. Keep the volume low: square, S&H and the ends of the ramps on the volume or the filters click by design, and stepped modulation can briefly push the output past full scale.

## Steps

1. **Save your logs first.** Copy `roms/ports/audreybrick/data/` from the card to the Mac (it holds your recordings too). Replacing the folder deletes it.
2. **Copy the two items** from `dist/AudreyBrick/` into `roms/ports`, replacing the old ones: `AudreyBrick.sh` and the folder `audreybrick`.
3. **Start AudreyBrick** with the volume low. Raise the feedback with **R2** until it drones. Check the tab bar: seven tabs, MOD last. Press **L1** once: you are on MOD. Five rows (TARGET, SHAPE, RATE, DEPTH, POLARITY), the cursor on TARGET showing NONE; all four cards say NO TARGET, are dimmed and show `+/-` beside their depth; card 1 has the bright frame and number. Hold **X**: the cards light up and the rows dim; press **right** a few times and watch the frame go round 1, 2, 3, 4, 1; **left** goes the other way. Leave it on slot 1 and release X.
4. **A slow sine on the frequency.** Slot 1, on TARGET: press **right** twice (FEEDBACK, FREQUENCY). **Down** twice (RATE): it shows 1.00 HZ; press **left** about 8 times (about 0.37 HZ). **Down** (DEPTH): press **right** 3 times (+6.0%). You should hear a slow vibrato. Go to LOOP (**R1**): FREQUENCY has an `M1` tag and a violet line moving under its bar; the number stays put. Note whether the tag and line are easy to see.
5. **The same at audio rate.** Back on MOD (**L1**; the cursor is still on DEPTH, as you left it), press **up** once to RATE: hold **right** until it reads several hundred HZ (watch the value climb; hold **SELECT** for fine). The vibrato turns into a buzzy, ring-modulated tone. Note the CPU cell's value and MAX.
6. **Slot 2, the target row and the other shapes.** Still on RATE: hold **X**, press **right**: slot 2 (its card gets the frame), release X. The cursor stays on RATE, now slot 2's (1.00 HZ). **Up** twice to TARGET (NONE): press **left** once: it goes round to DELAY SWITCH; press **right** once: back to NONE. Now **hold right** and watch it step through the list, 8 per second; release it, then tap **left** / **right** until it reads LOWPASS (FILTER is shown after the name; from NONE it is **right** four times: FEEDBACK, FREQUENCY, BODY, LOWPASS). **Down** once (SHAPE), **right** once (TRIANGLE); **down** twice (DEPTH), **right** 20 times (+40.0%). Try each shape on LOWPASS (up twice to SHAPE, left / right: SINE, TRIANGLE, SQUARE, RAMP UP, RAMP DOWN, EXP UP, EXP DOWN, S&H, ENVELOPE) with that depth, first at a slow RATE (0.5 to 3 HZ) and then fast (200 HZ and above). At a slow rate RAMP UP should sweep the filter up and snap back; EXP DOWN should snap open and close fast, then ever more slowly; EXP UP the reverse (a slow swell that speeds up, then the snap). Then, on DEPTH, hold **X** and press **left** and **right**: you land on slot 1's DEPTH (+6.0%) and back on slot 2's (+40.0%); the row stays DEPTH. Hold **X** and press **L1**: you go to INPUT and the cards are no longer lit; go back with **R1** (release X first).
7. **Polarity.** Still slot 2 on LOWPASS: SHAPE RAMP UP, RATE about 0.5 HZ, DEPTH +40%. **Down** to POLARITY (the last row): it reads BIPOLAR, and the filter sweeps from below the LOWPASS knob to above it. Press **right**: UNIPOLAR (the card shows `+`): now the sweep starts at the knob and only goes up; the change itself should not click. Set DEPTH to **-40%** (up to DEPTH, then **hold left** until it reads -40.0%; it is 40 taps): with UNIPOLAR the filter now only goes below the knob. Watch the card's bar: with UNIPOLAR it stays on one side of its centre line. Then set SHAPE to ENVELOPE: POLARITY shows UNIPOLAR dimmed with FIXED BY ENVELOPE, and left / right do nothing there. Back to an LFO shape, it is UNIPOLAR again (kept).
8. **Resets.** On slot 2 (up / down to reach each row): **Y + left** on POLARITY (BIPOLAR), **Y + left** on the DEPTH row (0.0%; the card goes dark), **Y + left** on RATE (1.00 HZ), **Y + left** on SHAPE (SINE), **Y + left** on TARGET (NONE). Set the target, shape, depth and polarity again, then hold **Y** and **X** together and press **left**: the whole slot is back to its start (BIPOLAR too), and slot 2 stays selected. Slot 1 should be untouched.
9. **Four slots at audio rate, watching for crackle.** Using **X + left / right** to move between slots (the row you are on stays, which helps here: set one row for all four slots, then the next row), set all four slots to SINE at 1.00 KHZ or more with DEPTH +50%: FREQUENCY, LOWPASS, TIME and FEEDBACK. Leave it droning for **3 minutes**. Listen for crackle or dropouts (as opposed to the modulation itself) and note the CPU cell (value and MAX) now and then. Then set the four depths back to 0.
10. **Envelope from the inputs, before the gain.** Slot 1 (**X + left / right** to select it): target LOWPASS, SHAPE ENVELOPE (the RATE row now reads RELEASE), DEPTH +60%. The envelope now listens to the input **before** the INPUT tab's gain: the gain changes what you hear of the input, not how far the filter moves.
   - **File:** on INPUT choose FILE and a file with clear attacks; the filter should open with each attack and close over the RELEASE time. Try RELEASE short (right makes it longer, left shorter) and long. Then turn the INPUT gain down to **-inf dB**: you no longer hear the file, but **the filter still follows it**, as strongly as before. Raise the gain again: the filter's movement should not change with it.
   - **Microphone (headphones on):** LIVE, open `audiocodec, `. When a device opens for the first time its gain is at -inf dB: leave it there. Watch the PRE bar move when you tap the Brick or speak: **with the gain at -inf, the filter still follows the mic**, and nothing of the mic is heard. Then raise the gain a little to check you hear the mic, and lower it to -inf again.
   - **USB:** the same with your USB interface, starting at -inf dB on its first open.
   - With the source OFF the envelope does nothing (the card's bar stays still), whatever the gain.
11. **Modulated delay switch.** Slot 3: target DELAY SWITCH, SQUARE, RATE about 0.5 HZ, DEPTH +100%. The switch box should turn dark with a green frame, ON and MOD in green, every other second, and the delay should warp in time with it. Press **B** while it is off and while it is on: B still works. **Select + B** latches it as before. Set slot 3's DEPTH to 0.
12. **Volume and feedback.** With UNIPOLAR, modulation moves only to one side of the knob. Slot 4: target VOLUME, TRIANGLE, 0.2 HZ, DEPTH **-50%**, POLARITY **UNIPOLAR**: with VOLUME at its start-up 0.50 the sound should swell down to silence and back up to the knob's level, never louder, without clicks. Keep the headphone level low. Afterwards set the DEPTH back to 0 and POLARITY to BIPOLAR. Then target FEEDBACK (on TARGET, **left** from VOLUME ten times, or **right** from NONE once), SINE, 0.1 HZ, DEPTH +20% with the feedback just below where the drone starts on its own: the loop should come and go (it also swings above the stored feedback, so start low). Set DEPTH back to 0 when done.
13. **Input gain (headphones on).** With LIVE open and the gain around -6 dB: slot 4 target INPUT GAIN, SINE, 1 HZ, DEPTH +30%. The GAIN row gets a tag and a violet line; the large meter should rise and fall with the modulation. Then lower the gain to **-inf dB** with d-pad down: the input must stay silent although the slot still modulates. **Warning, if you also try ENVELOPE on INPUT GAIN:** with a positive depth the envelope now boosts a turned-down input strongly (it no longer shrinks with the gain: at -12 dB an envelope of 0.3 with depth +100 % adds about +13.7 dB), and with the speaker and the microphone that is a loop that feeds on its own level and can howl. Keep headphones on and the volume low, or use a negative depth (ducking). At exactly -inf dB the input stays silent whatever the modulation.
14. **Recording while modulating.** With two or three slots running, record about 30 seconds with **Start + Y** (on the MOD tab and on another tab). Copy the file off later and check that it sounds like what you heard.
15. **Quit** with Menu + Start.
16. **Send back** the `roms/ports/audreybrick/data/` folder **without** the `recordings` folder (logs only: `audreybrick*.log`, `stdout*.log`), and your notes. Send the recording from step 14 only if it sounds different from what you heard.

## What the logs will show (for me)

- A `mod: slot ...` line each time a slot's settings have stayed unchanged for half a second (shape, target, rate or release, depth, and ` unipolar` at the end for a unipolar LFO), so the log shows what you played. Selecting a slot with X is not logged (it changes no settings).
- The statistics line every 10 seconds as before (callback load, CPU frequency, overruns): step 9 is the one that matters for cost.

## Things I would like your opinion on

- **Step sizes:** RATE moves 13 % per tap and ramps at the knobs' speed; DEPTH moves 2 % per tap. Too coarse, too fine, too slow to get from slow drift to audio rate?
- **Readability:** are the five rows and the four (now shorter) cards readable at a glance? Is the `+/-` / `+` beside the depth enough to tell the polarity? Are the violet tags and the moving line on the other tabs visible enough, or too busy?
- **Polarity:** is UNIPOLAR useful as it is (the knob is one end of the movement), and is POLARITY as a fifth row the right place for it?
- **The ramps:** are RAMP UP / DOWN and EXP UP / DOWN useful, and is the exponential's curve (half its travel in the first seventh of the cycle) about right?
- **RELEASE direction:** right makes the release longer (the number goes up). Does that feel right, or should right mean "faster" as on RATE?
- **Holding X for slots:** is holding X and pressing left / right a comfortable way to pick the slot? Is one step per press right, or should it repeat when held?
- **The selected card:** with the cards dimmed, is it obvious which slot the rows edit (the bright frame and number)? Can you tell an active slot (lighter card, moving bar) from an idle one while dimmed?
- **Targets without a list:** are 14 targets on left / right (going round, 8 per second when held) enough, or do you miss the list?
- Anything in the gestures that felt awkward.
